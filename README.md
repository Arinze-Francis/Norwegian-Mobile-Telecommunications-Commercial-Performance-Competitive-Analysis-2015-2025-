**Norwegian-Mobile-Telecommunications-Commercial-Performance-Competitive-Analysis-2015-2025**


**Using Python notebook** in Fabric, the goal of the analysis is to analyze and understand:  
1. Who is winning in the Norwegian telecom market?
2. What is driving their performance?
3. Where are the commercial risks?

The commercial questions to answer are:

1. Which operators generated the most revenue between 2015 and 2025?

2. What percentage of the market's cumulative revenue each operator generated.

3. How is the revenue growing?

4. What is the subscription volumes by operator?

5. How is the subscription growing?

6. Is growth stronger in consumer or business markets?

7. Which mobile operators are most dependent on the slower-growing business segment?

🔍 Key Findings

📈 Revenue: Total reported revenue increased by 28.1%, with annual growth reaching 4.9% in 2025.

📱 Subscriptions: The subscription base grew by 7.1%, significantly slower than revenue.

🏢 Market Competition: Telenor and Telia remained dominant, but their combined annual revenue share declined by 15.1 percentage points, while their subscription share fell by 20.4 percentage points.

💡 Customer Segments: Consumer revenue grew substantially faster than business revenue, contributing most of the market's revenue expansion.

⚠️ Data Limitations

Historical reporting changes, particularly involving ice and Lyse Tele, affect operator-level comparisons.

Key Takeaway: Revenue growth has outpaced subscription expansion, while the competitive landscape has shifted beyond the traditional market leaders.
